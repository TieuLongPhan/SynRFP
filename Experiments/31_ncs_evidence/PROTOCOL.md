# NCS evidence analysis v1

This new sidecar reads completed reports without modifying experiment sources,
historical reports, raw data, feature caches, splits or model weights. It trains
no models, starts no encoders and uses no GPU. Its outputs are exploratory
evidence for discussion, not an automatic preset selection or a manuscript edit.

## Inputs and admission

The initial report requires all completed experiment22 classification (5,040),
clustering (4,320) and yield (4,608) cells. Each task COMPLETE receipt must bind
the unchanged plan and every listed report output, including exact CSV bytes.
EHReact is excluded. Every native partition requires the exact72 configurations
with equal optimizer-seed populations across constructions; duplicate, missing,
nonfinite, incomplete and changed cells fail admission.

The report remains explicitly preliminary until experiment30 has published
COMMON_TASK_ANALYSIS_COMPLETE.json. That receipt must attest20448 logical cells,
72 configurations and allseven tasks, bind its four source ledgers and bind its
own output files. The new6,480 cells must match that attestation and their own
canonical metric/artifact completion ledger. No partial30 rows enter the analysis.

## Prespecified paired effects

At fixed tokenizer, radius, sketch, source endpoint, evaluation regime and native
partition, report these differences:

- delta_union minus delta
- roles minus delta
- roles minus delta_union
- union minus delta

The factor grid is WL/Morgan/Path × radius2/3 × parity/MinHash/controlledCW ×
delta/union/delta_union/originalroles, with2048 total bits. No retired jointroles
variant is admitted. The source plans, rather than this report, define the
scientific encoder recipes. At full count level U=|D|+2S; the role/context
comparison concerns support, sketches and bit allocation after this identity.

Optimizer seeds within one condition fold are averaged first. Native reagent,
native condition and FREA initializations share fixed test populations and are
also averaged first. Clustering initialization comparisons remain explicitly
identified; they are not cross-validation. Yield native partitions and barrier
grouped folds/holdouts retain their original identities.

Endpoints average paired partition differences. Related endpoints average inside
source families; source-family means form task summaries. Native and grouped
reagent/condition views remain separate. Yield regimes average within families.
GDB7/RDB7 share a declared related family; E2/SN2/combined E2SN2 share another.
Metrics remain task-specific. Barrier raw RMSE differences retain their sign;
a separate benefit column reverses that sign. All other benefit columns retain
their raw score difference. Correlated-partition standard deviations are
descriptive; no p-values, independent-replication counts or confidence intervals
are produced. Historical selection remains exploratory even with more seeds.

## Reused explanatory evidence

Inventory and hash existing experiment12 cluster-coherence summaries,
representative-selection cells, construction-attribution summaries, locality and
collision tables, cost tables and mapped bond-coherence tables. The inventory
records evidence as it exists; it is not a rerun of those experiments or a new
chemical identity proof. Report the168-cell representative-selection study's
20,000-row samples, two datasets, three seeds, four budgets and missing crxnfp.
Retain mixed results and coarse-label saturation. Legacy WL/r2/1024/delta
measurements do not validate every new2048-bit mode or sketch.

Existing cost results and native fit durations are descriptive under their own
settings and load. Do not run the idle-machine throughput protocol while current
research queues are busy. No inference about completion of YieldBERT, GNAN,
SynCat or any other learned reference follows from this analysis marker.

## Execution, outputs and completion

`python Experiments/31_ncs_evidence/analysis.py --once` writes derived outputs
under `publication_metadata/ncs_evidence_v1`. Local state and its run manifest
live under `~/.local/share/synrfp-runs/ncs_evidence_v1`. `--root` and `--output`
permit isolated validation. The worker has no wait loop. A capped controller can
run once now and again after the common seven-task receipt appears.

Outputs include matched, endpoint, family and task contrast CSVs; the evidence
inventory and summary; source identities; a priority report and JSON status.
Unchanged inputs reuse verified derived outputs. Atomic output replacement is
confined to this new report directory. `COMPLETE.json` means the seven-task
descriptive analysis is complete; it never means all references or NCS research
are finished. Optional token-audit/reference status receipts are displayed only
as their own scoped evidence and do not change factorial admission.

When a controller supplies both `NCS_EVIDENCE_STAGE_ID` (32 lowercase hexadecimal
characters) and `NCS_EVIDENCE_INPUT_KEY`, successful execution, including an
unchanged-input reuse, publishes an attempt-scoped `PRODUCER_READY.json` under
the local `stages/<id>/` directory. It binds the stage identity, controller input
key, producer script SHA and exact output status bytes. These environment values
route execution evidence and have no role in the scientific calculations.
