# Earlier BitBIRCH results

The CSV collates 36 original results covering 12 endpoints and three binary fingerprints. The threshold for each fingerprint was calibrated by AMI on balanced USPTO50K and held fixed on the other endpoints. The SynRFP fingerprint uses WL radius 2, parity, signed delta, and 1,024 bits. The CSV records the exact source blobs and defines singleton fraction as the fraction of reactions assigned to singleton clusters.

The Request 79 channel comparison uses a 2,048-bit budget and five matched input orders. Its results have their own tables.
